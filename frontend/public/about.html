About GameSummoner

Why this exists

GameSummoner is a passion project. I built this because it's something I wanted to exist. The premise is simple: describe a game the way you'd describe it to a friend, and a little while later a real, playable game exists. Every game on the demos page was made by the machine from the words shown under it.

Meet the founder

I'm Nick Damiano, and I built GameSummoner because I believe AI can create amazing things, and games are one of them. The pieces all existed already; LLMs to write code, image, sound and 3D models to generate assets. What was missing was putting them together in a way that does meaningful work.

Privacy is structural, not a policy

I value privacy, so the service is built in a way that doesn't depend on any model provider. By default, generation runs on open-weight models on GPUs I rent and operate myself, and your prompts are never sent to any third-party AI provider. Outside models make better games today, so you can choose Quality for a game, which sends it to one; that choice is yours, game by game, and Private is where every account starts. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, no ads, and your data is never sold or shared. The site sets one essential cookie, which signs your browser into the game player. The privacy note is short because there isn't much to disclose.

How it stays alive

GPU time costs real money, and nothing subsidizes it. There are no ads, no investors, no data resale. Credits exist so that I can pay for the compute that actually makes the game. That's the whole business model.
