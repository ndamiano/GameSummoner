GameSummoner — Privacy Note

Effective 2026-11-04

What we store

  • Account: your handle, your email address, your password in hashed form, your credit balance and history.
  • Purchases: which credit package you bought, the amount, and the payment provider's reference for the payment. Card details are entered on the provider's checkout page and never reach us.
  • What you create: your requests, the game designs drafted from them, your change requests, and the generated games and their assets.
  • Operations: logs needed to run, debug, and protect the Service: build activity, resource usage, content-safety screening results, and standard server logs.

Private and Quality games

Each game is made one of two ways, chosen when you summon it. New accounts start on Quality, which sends your game to an outside AI provider; you can switch any game to Private when you summon it, or make Private your default in Settings.

  • Private: every AI model that works on the game runs on GPUs we rent and operate ourselves. Your requests, designs and games are not sent to any outside AI provider.
  • Quality: the language models that write the design and the code, and that check the running game, are an outside provider's, currently DeepSeek, run by Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd. Your request, the design, the game's code, your change requests and screenshots of the game are sent to them. Their own privacy policy governs what they keep and whether they learn from it. Images and 3D models are still made on our GPUs.

A game keeps the choice it was made with, through every later change and fix.

What we use your email for

Signing in, resetting your password, and contacting you about your account. Nothing else; no newsletters, no marketing.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data, except with the infrastructure that runs the Service, the payment provider that processes purchases, the AI provider of a game you made on Quality, and where the law requires it.

Cookies

One essential cookie signs your browser into the game player. No tracking cookies.

Retention and removal

Data is kept while your account exists. To remove your account and its data, mail support@gamesummoner.com from the address on the account.
