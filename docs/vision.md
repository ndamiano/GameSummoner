# GameSummoner — Vision and Non-Goals

## The goal

Someone describes the game in their head, and a while later they are playing it.

That is the whole product. A person who cannot code, or cannot spare the years, says what they want
and gets a real game back. It opens in a browser: no install, no launcher, no store approval, no
build step between the maker and the player.

AI quality is the product. The absolute best user experience in the world doesn't save a bad game.

## What success looks like

### Breadth and quality

Success looks like typing in "make me an open world game with dragons" and a game with the quality
and scale of Skyrim comes out. It looks like an artist with an extremely detailed vision gets the
game they described, and can easily tweak it where it's not what they imagined.

### Exists independent of anyone else

GameSummoner runs on hardware we own, open-weight models, and open-source software. Nothing in the
path from prompt to game can be taken away by a vendor.

### Worth it, every time

When someone shows up and spends a few dollars on a game, they get a game they want to play. The
price is right, and they end up satisfied and feeling like they got their money's worth.

### Privacy

GameSummoner will not track users, will not sell user data, and will not use third party inference
unless the user chooses it. The default never leaves our hardware.

## Non-goals

### Not a general purpose agent

GameSummoner is designed to make games, and do it well. Even if it writes code or creates music, it
will only do those things in service of making a game.

### GameSummoner is not *your* agent

GameSummoner doesn't learn about you. It doesn't get to know you or your preferences. It simply
takes what you give it and turns it into a game.

### Not optimized for speed

While it would be amazing if GameSummoner could turn out games in milliseconds, GameSummoner will
always prefer higher quality games. If that can also be gained at the same time as a speed boost,
great, but the preference is game quality.
