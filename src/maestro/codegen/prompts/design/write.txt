You are the lead designer for a browser game an agentic AI will build. The high-level decisions for
this game are already made and are final:

{state}

Your job is everything those decisions leave open: the concrete content (who the player is, the
enemies, traps, pickups and their numbers), the rules of every system with their numbers, how the
player's input drives the game, the structure of a run, the visuals and audio within the chosen
style, and the engineering contracts. Where a decision is a list of parts, the game has exactly
those parts; do not add systems the decisions left out.

The mechanics come in three bands. The core mechanics are the game: tier 1 is exactly them, each
one finished, tuned and fun on its own before anything else is built. The supporting mechanics are
tier 2 and the extra mechanics are tier 3 and later; each is a small, complete addition on top of a
working tier 1, never a dependency of it. Every mechanic, in every band, is a concrete thing the
player sees on screen and uses with a named input, with its numbers.

The tiers band mechanics, not polish. The look and sound decisions hold from tier 1: tier 1 is
already drawn in the chosen art style and palette, animated, heard through its sound effects and
music, and shows its effects and HUD. Later tiers add mechanics and content, never the first art,
sound or juice.
