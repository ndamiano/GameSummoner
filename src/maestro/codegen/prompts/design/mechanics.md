# Mechanics

Every row is asked on its own, yes/no, as "name: description". Headings are for reading only.

| Mechanic | Description |
|---|---|

| chase and catch | the player pursues moving targets that run away and grabs them |
| flee | the player runs from something that pursues them |
| dodge | the player avoids incoming attacks or projectiles by moving |
| sneak | the player moves quietly to avoid being noticed |
| push and pull | the player shoves or drags blocks and objects around |
| carry and tow | the player hauls an object or vehicle from one place to another |
| pick up and drop | the player grabs items from the world and puts them down elsewhere |
| place | the player puts pieces, towers or tiles onto the map |
| dig | the player digs or mines through the ground |
| grapple and swing | the player attaches a rope or hook and swings from it |
| fly | the player moves freely through the air |
| swim | the player moves through water |
| steer | the player turns and drives a moving vehicle or creature |
| launch | the player sets angle and power, then fires something into a physics arc |
| bounce | the player or a ball rebounds off surfaces |
| match | the player lines up or groups identical tiles to clear them |
| swap and rotate | the player swaps or rotates pieces to fit a pattern |
| merge | the player combines two identical things into a bigger one |
| sort | the player arranges items into the right order or bins |

| type | the player types words or letters on the keyboard |

| grow | the player plants things and tends them as they grow |
| herd and lead | the player guides a group of creatures or people to a place |
| escort and rescue | the player protects or frees someone and brings them to safety |
| defend a point | the player stops enemies from reaching or destroying something |

| bet and bluff | the player wagers or hides information from opponents |
| memorize | the player remembers patterns, sequences or layouts |

| paint and colour | the player fills areas or objects with colour |
